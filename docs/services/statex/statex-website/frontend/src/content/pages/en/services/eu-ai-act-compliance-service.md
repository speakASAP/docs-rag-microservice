# EU AI Act Compliance Services

## Make Your AI Systems Compliant with EU Regulation

Statex helps European companies achieve and maintain full compliance with the EU AI Act. We combine deep technical expertise in AI systems with practical regulatory experience, delivering end‑to‑end support from initial audit to implementation of all required controls and documentation.

### 🧭 What We Deliver

- **Complete AI System Inventory** – mapping all AI use cases, models, and data flows
- **Risk Classification** – identifying high‑risk, limited‑risk, and minimal‑risk AI systems
- **Gap Analysis** – comparing your current state with EU AI Act requirements
- **Implementation Roadmap** – prioritized, realistic compliance plan
- **Documentation Package** – policies, technical documentation, logs, and records
- **Ongoing Support** – periodic reviews, re‑assessments, and updates

## Our EU AI Act Compliance Approach

### 1. Discovery & System Inventory

- Identify all AI and automated decision‑making systems in your organization
- Map data sources, processing pipelines, and downstream dependencies
- Classify systems by business domain (finance, HR, operations, customer‑facing, etc.)
- Identify third‑party AI services and SaaS dependencies

### 2. Risk Classification & Regulatory Mapping

- Classify each AI system according to EU AI Act risk tiers
- Identify prohibited practices and ensure they are not present
- Map applicable requirements for high‑risk and limited‑risk systems
- Highlight overlaps with GDPR, cybersecurity, and sector regulations

### 3. Compliance Gap Analysis

- Review **technical controls**: robustness, monitoring, logging, fallback mechanisms
- Review **data governance**: data quality, bias mitigation, retention, access control
- Review **governance & processes**: roles, responsibilities, change management
- Review **human oversight**: escalation paths, review procedures, override mechanisms

### 4. Implementation & Remediation

We do not only write reports – we help you implement the required changes:

- Design or improve **AI risk management framework**
- Implement or tune **monitoring, logging, and audit trails**
- Define **human‑in‑the‑loop decision flows** for high‑risk use cases
- Implement **model lifecycle management** (training, validation, deployment, rollback)
- Update **policies, procedures, and internal guidelines**

### 5. Documentation & Audit Readiness

- Create or update **technical documentation** for each relevant AI system
- Prepare **compliance evidence** (logs, reports, test results, DPIAs where relevant)
- Provide **user‑facing transparency notices** and explanations for AI decisions
- Support internal and external auditors with structured documentation packages

### 6. Continuous Compliance

- Set up **regular review cycles** for AI systems and risk classification
- Monitor regulatory updates and best practices
- Provide **training for business, tech, and compliance teams**
- Offer ongoing advisory for new AI initiatives and vendors
